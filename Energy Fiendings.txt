SMART CITY ENERGY – KEY INSIGHTS

1. The North Zone shows the highest total energy consumption, indicating higher load concentration and possible infrastructure stress.

2. Industrial consumers contribute the largest share of total kWh usage, significantly exceeding residential and commercial segments.

3. Monthly trend analysis reveals a steady increase in consumption during mid-year months, suggesting seasonal demand impact.

4. Zones with higher outage minutes also show elevated peak usage levels, indicating potential grid instability during high-demand periods.

5. Average cost per zone closely correlates with tariff rate variations, with Central Zone generating the highest estimated revenue.

6. Weekend peak usage is lower than weekday peak usage for commercial consumers, while residential usage remains relatively stable.

7. Certain meters consistently report higher outage durations and fault status, suggesting maintenance prioritization is required.
